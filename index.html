AI Safety for Young People · Available 2027

Jake Chanenson

I study how to make AI systems safer for young people. Children's privacy scholar, law-informed HCI researcher.

I'm a CS PhD researcher at the University of Chicago working on AI safety for young people with Marshini Chetty at the Amyoli Internet Research Lab. My work asks three questions: who controls young people's information, how institutions govern AI use, and when young people actually want AI assistance. Children's privacy is the throughline. It is where AI governance is being written first, through COPPA rulemaking, state chatbot statutes, and school district policy, and where the distance between what the law requires and what young people need is widest. I combine empirical HCI, computational methods, and legal analysis, and I hold a Master of Legal Studies from UChicago Law, so I can see both what the law protects and where the gaps are. Finishing my PhD in spring 2027. My work spans research, law, and policy, and I am drawn to roles across academia, industry, and government where that breadth is useful.
